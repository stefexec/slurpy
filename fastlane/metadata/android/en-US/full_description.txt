Slurpy is a free, privacy-friendly, open-source companion app and precision countdown timer for concentrates and quartz bangers.

Features:
• Precision Heating & Cooling Phases: Prominent countdown timer with smooth visual arc progress and synthesized audio alert chimes.
• Customizable Presets: Tailor heat-up and cool-down times, temperature ranges, names, and emojis. Reorder or reset anytime.
• Celsius & Fahrenheit Support: Easily switch temperature units across all presets and steppers in settings.
• Activity History & Trend Calendar: View logged sessions, all-time weekly distribution bar charts, and a compact 4-week activity heatmap with week-over-week trend insights.
• Swipe to Delete: Manage session logs with swipe-to-reveal delete and a 5-second undo recovery notice.
• Banger Cleaning Guide: Built-in step-by-step care and maintenance manual to preserve heat retention and flavor.
• Multiple Dark Themes: Choose between different nice looking color schemes.
• 100% Offline & Private: No trackers, no ads, no internet permissions, no data collection.
